What's inside

  • Bold, simple outlines Big shapes that little hands can color, on 8.5 × 11 inch pages.
  • One side only Each picture is printed on one side, with a blank back. Using markers? Slip a sheet of scrap paper behind the page.
  • Games to play Every book has at least one maze, plus matching and find-it games. Books 2 to 7 add spot-the-difference pages and a helper certificate to finish.
  • Pets and wild animals Meet pets up close in every book. Books 3 to 7 also meet wild animals, with safety tips along the way.

Paws and Learn

Look for the Paws and Learn signs in every book, and Vet Tips in Books 2 to 7. They are short, true facts for a grown-up to read aloud.

A veterinarian checks every one. Want to know where a fact came from? See the sources for each book.

About Wee Vets Press

Wee Vets Press makes coloring books about real animals for young kids. Each book follows a group of pets and the people who care for them. Books 3 to 7 also meet wild animals, from rattlesnakes to whale sharks.

The facts in our books are checked by a veterinarian, and the sources behind them are listed on our sources page. The books are made to be fun first, and to help kids feel calm and curious about animals and vet visits.
